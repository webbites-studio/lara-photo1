About Lara

An eye trained on stillness.

For over a decade, Lara has photographed people and places with the same quiet attention — searching for the unguarded moment rather than the perfect one. Her work moves between fashion editorial, documentary wedding coverage, and personal travel projects, unified by a restrained, cinematic sense of light.
