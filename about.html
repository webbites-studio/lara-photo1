About

Lara Nikolau

"I've always been more interested in the moment before the smile than the smile itself."

Lara is a Paris-based photographer working across portraiture, weddings, fashion, and editorial commission. Her practice is built on patience — spending long, unhurried hours observing light and gesture before a single frame is made. The result is a body of work that feels less composed than discovered.

Originally trained in fine art painting before turning to the camera, she brings a painter's sense of tone and restraint to every shoot — favoring natural light, honest expression, and a muted, timeless palette over trend.

Philosophy

How I See

01

Light First

Every session begins with the light, not the pose. Direction, quality, and time of day dictate the entire shoot.

02

Unposed Moments

Direction is quiet and minimal — most of my favorite frames happen in the pause between instructions.

03

Restraint in Editing

A muted, consistent tone across every gallery. Nothing is over-processed; the goal is to feel timeless, not trendy.
